# Fixture photos

Photos from Wikimedia Commons, resized to 1280 px wide, used to test the
engines. Each keeps its own licence (below), not the licence of the code.

The `*_ref.png` reference masks were derived by machine (BiRefNet lite, or
MODNet + IS-Net), then cleaned by hand. A mask is an adaptation of its photo
and is shared under the photo's licence: CC BY-SA 2.0 for `rainy_ref.png`.
